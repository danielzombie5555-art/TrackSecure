ACCESS / SAFETY NOTE
Some Tawau locations have current-access uncertainty or private/forestry restrictions. Sin Onn Hill, Lim Man Kui and Tiger Hill especially should be checked locally before visiting. Bukit Panchang requires current forestry/guide arrangements according to the published source. Tawau Hills Park mountain climbs require registration, permits and guides according to Sabah Parks.

The app does not claim an exact GPS line for a trail unless the source supports it.
